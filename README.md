## ✨ Featured Projects & Case Studies

### 1. 📚 Kids Book — AI-Powered Bilingual Learning App for Children
- **Category:** Mobile Development (Flutter) • AI Integration • EdTech
- **Status:** Working Prototype Published on GitHub | In Active Development
- **Problem Solved:** Millions of children in Bangladesh (aged 6–9) have smartphone access but lack structured, native language digital early learning tools.
- **Solution:** A bilingual (Bangla & English) self-learning application for Class 1–3 children with an AI-powered adaptive content delivery layer.
- **Stack:** Flutter, Kotlin, Python, AI Adaptive Layer, Android Studio, Git.

### 2. 💍 Digital Wedding Planning Platform (Bangladesh)
- **Category:** Product Strategy • 82-Feature MVP Specification
- **Status:** Planning Phase | MVP Specification Complete
- **Problem Solved:** Wedding planning in Bangladesh remains fragmented and unorganized with no unified coordination system.
- **Solution:** Wrote an 82-feature MVP specification covering workspace permissions, guest management, and invitation tokens, backed by a comprehensive pre-launch risk and gap analysis.
- **Stack:** Product Thinking, MVP Specification, System Design, Gap Analysis, Figma.

### 3. 🌾 Aabadi — D2C E-Commerce & Consumer Traceability
- **Category:** D2C Food Brand • Web E-Commerce • Traceability
- **Status:** In Development
- **Problem Solved:** Lack of consumer trust and widespread adulteration in Bangladesh's packaged cooking essentials.
- **Solution:** Sourcing cold-pressed mustard oil directly from Jamalpur farmers for urban Dhaka homemakers, featuring consumer-facing batch traceability for authenticity verification.
- **Stack:** JavaScript, HTML5/CSS3, PHP / Python, SQL, D2C Supply Chain.
