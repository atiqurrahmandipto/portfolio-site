Who I Am

I’m a CSE (Software Engineering) graduate from East West University, based in Dhaka. I’m a fresher, and I don’t see that as something I need to hide. What I may lack in years of experience, I make up for with curiosity, hands-on work, and a habit of figuring things out properly.

I enjoy taking real problems, breaking them down, understanding what actually matters, and turning ideas into something useful whether that means building a software product, researching a solution, or thinking through the strategy behind it.

I work across both the technical and problem solving sides of a project. I’m comfortable writing code, working with databases and tools, exploring product ideas, and figuring out how different pieces fit together.

I also use AI tools like Cursor, GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, and Antigravity as part of my workflow. They help me research faster, explore ideas, and iterate more efficiently but I don’t treat AI as a substitute for thinking. I still believe the important part is knowing what to ask, questioning the output, testing assumptions, catching gaps, and making the final decisions myself.

Right now, I’m exploring opportunities in both software development and product / strategy. They may look like different paths, but for me they come from the same place: I like understanding problems, building things, and figuring out how to make them better.

I’m still at the beginning of my career and that’s exactly what makes me excited about what comes next.
